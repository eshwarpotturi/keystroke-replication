Record iPhone taps (on this iPhone)

Open this page in Safari on your iPhone and keep it on screen the whole time — don't switch apps, recording stops if Safari goes to the background. Keep "Keyboard Clicks" sound ON (Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Keyboard Feedback).

Tap the text box below to bring up your keyboard, then tap the key shown on that keyboard. Nothing you type is read or stored — the box is only there to open the keyboard. Taps are found afterwards from the sound, same as the separate-mic version.

Send the downloaded file back in the chat (use the Files app or AirDrop/share to get it off the phone). Same format as the separate-mic version.

Takes about 10–15 minutes for 36 keys. Do it somewhere reasonably quiet. For digits, tap the "123" key on your keyboard to switch, then tap the digit, same as normal typing.
