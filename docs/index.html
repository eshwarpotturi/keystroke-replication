Can a phone hear what you type?

A replication of "A Practical Deep Learning-Based Acoustic Side Channel Attack on Keyboards" (Harrison, Toreini, Mehrnezhad, 2023) on the authors' own public recordings.

Try it: type something, see what a phone hears

Letters a-z and digits 0-9. Uses key presses the model never saw in training, from the authors' recordings. It does not listen to your audio.
