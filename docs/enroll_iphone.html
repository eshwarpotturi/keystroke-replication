Record iPhone taps (separate mic)

This runs on your listening device (this laptop) — its mic records while you tap on your iPhone, held or placed near it, like the original phone/Zoom setup. Keep the iPhone's "Keyboard Clicks" sound ON (Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Keyboard Feedback). You'll need to switch the iPhone to its number pad (123) when asked for a digit.

No connection between the two devices is needed or possible — this page can't see what you tap. It just records continuously and finds each tap from the sound afterwards, the same way the original attack works.
